Who's behind it

Driven by precision.
Built for performance.

3Dynamics bridges the gap between high-level mechanical engineering and competitive sim racing. Founded by Athanasios Antonios, every peripheral is designed from the ground up using topology optimization, custom embedded firmware and rapid additive manufacturing — built for relentless feedback, durability and immersion.

  • 100% in-house R&D — every part is modeled in Fusion 360, run through structural stress analysis, and printed on high-precision CoreXY machines.
  • Plug-and-play compatibility — recognized natively as a standard USB HID controller, no drivers or extra setup. Works with Assetto Corsa, iRacing, DiRT Rally and BeamNG.drive.
  • Open to custom modding — designed by engineers, for enthusiasts. Easy to disassemble, maintain and integrate into your own rig.
